Temporary Suspension of Mobile Library Services – Mobile Library 5 [Service Suspension Date: 18-20.07.2026]


20.07.2026

Due to urgent maintenance of the library van, the library service of Mobile Library 5 will be suspended on the following date.  We apologise for any inconvenience this may cause.

The mobile library service point affected is:

Service Point Suspension Date
Adjacent to Yiu Ping House, Yiu On Estate, Ma On Shan 18.07.2026 (Sat)
Adjacent to Hin Yeung House, Hin Keng Estate, Sha Tin 20.07.2026 (Mon)

Readers are welcome to use other public libraries during the service suspension. They may also renew library materials by telephone at 2698 0002 or 2827 2833 or via Internet at www.hkpl.gov.hk and “My Library” Mobile App.

For enquiries, please call  2696 5842.
