Temporary Suspension of Mobile Library Services – Mobile Library 4 [Service Suspension Date: 24.07.2026-25.07.2026]


20.07.2026

Due to urgent maintenance of the mobile library vehicle, Mobile Library 4 will suspend services from 24 July 2026 to 25 July 2026.  We apologise for any inconvenience this may cause.

The service points to be affected are as follows:

Service Point Suspension Date

Pui O

24.07.2026 (Friday)

Shui Hau

25.07.2026 (Saturday) a.m.

Tong Fuk

25.07.2026 (Saturday) p.m.

During the above period, readers who need to borrow or return library materials may visit other public libraries.  Loan items can also be renewed by telephone on 2698 0002 / 2827 2833 or online at http://www.hkpl.gov.hk.

For enquiries, please contact our mobile library staff on 2984 9417.
